## Full description (en)

You're a tiny, very hungry monster. The town is full of snacks.

Drag anywhere to move and gobble everything smaller than you: people, benches, bins and
flowers. Every bite makes you bigger - big enough for trees and cars, then buses and
fountains, then whole houses and skyscrapers!

But you're not the only monster in town. Rivals are growing too. Eat the ones smaller
than you, run from the bigger ones, and be the biggest monster when the timer runs out.

- Fast 100-second rounds against rival monsters
- 5 towns to devour: Tiny Town, Sunny Beach, Happy Farm, Snowy Village and Big City
- 14 cute monsters to collect
- Upgrades: start size, speed, round time, suction and coin bonus
- Power-ups: speed boost, magnet mouth and double growth
- Daily missions, a daily gift streak and a lucky wheel
- Level up to unlock new maps and monsters
- Plays offline
